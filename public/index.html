HOW IT WORKS

It’s not transaction scoring. It’s network detection.

Legacy batch review can inspect yesterday’s ledger. MuleShield is designed around the seconds in which stolen funds are being layered.

01

Stream

Payment events arrive through APIs or Kafka and are normalized into a common transaction shape.

02

Map

Accounts become graph nodes. Transactions become time-stamped edges, creating a living money-flow network.

03

Score

Behavioral signals, ML probability, and graph analytics combine into an explainable risk score.

04

Respond

Configurable thresholds route events to monitoring, step-up verification, review, or authorized restriction workflows.

ENGINE

Three layers. One explainable decision.

Built for investigators who need the “why”, not just a red number.

◉

Behavioral signals

Velocity, pass-through timing, fan-in/fan-out, amount splitting, new beneficiaries, account age, and transaction timing.

◇

Machine learning

XGBoost or scikit-learn models estimate transaction-level risk from engineered temporal and account features.

✦

Graph analytics

Network depth, centrality, expansion rate, connected exposure, and risk propagation reveal coordinated movement.

FAQ

Digital payments, explained.

What is a mule account?

A mule account is an account used to receive, move, or layer funds on behalf of a fraud operation. Legitimate accounts can also be exposed to suspicious flows, so network exposure should not automatically be treated as proof of wrongdoing.

Why use graph analytics?

Fraudulent movement can look ordinary at the individual transaction level. A graph reveals relationships, rapid expansion, fan-in/fan-out, repeated pass-through, and multi-hop money trails.

Does a high score automatically freeze an account?

No. The prototype uses configurable response bands. Real restrictions or holds should remain subject to the participating institution’s controls, authorization, and applicable procedures.

Where does the demo data come from?

The hackathon prototype uses synthetic transaction scenarios so the team can demonstrate known patterns without exposing real customer financial data.

Is this connected to the 1930 helpline or government systems?

No. This showcase does not claim direct integration with the National Cybercrime Reporting Portal, 1930 Helpline, banks, UPI, or crypto providers. Such integrations would require authorization and technical agreements.
